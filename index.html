About Me

I am a visual artist, painter and draftsman from Santa Fe de Antioquia, Colombia. My work is rooted in the experiential development of social and spiritual reflections through painting and drawing.

My works are inspired by realism, taking classical and baroque artistic traditions as primary influences. I encourage dialogue and questioning around everyday aspects of the social landscape, rethinking the meaning of our ethnic roots that shape our geographical and historical identity.

Technique

Acrylic on canvas

Style

Classical and baroque realism

Specialty

Portraits and decorative works
